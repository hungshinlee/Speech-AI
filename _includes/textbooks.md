## 主要教科書

| 代號 | 出處 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| **JM3** | Jurafsky & Martin, *Speech and Language Processing*, 3rd ed. **online draft, 2026-08-19 release**（<https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/>） | 語音與對話章節的教學語言基準；有官方 slides 可改 |

| **YD** | Yu & Deng, *Automatic Speech Recognition: A Deep Learning Approach*, Springer, 2015 | hybrid DNN-HMM 到 sequence discriminative training |
| **TAN** | Xu Tan, *Neural Text-to-Speech Synthesis*, Springer, 2023 `[驗]` | TTS 的系統性整理（章節編號請以實書為準） |
| **LOI** | Loizou, *Speech Enhancement: Theory and Practice*, 2nd ed., CRC Press, 2013 | enhancement 的統計方法與評估指標 |
| **VVG** | Vincent, Virtanen & Gannot, *Audio Source Separation and Speech Enhancement*, Wiley, 2018 | 分離與多通道處理 |
| **BB** | Bishop & Bishop, *Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts*, Springer, 2024 | diffusion / normalizing flow / VAE 的數學底 |
